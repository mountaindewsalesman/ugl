    Tokenizer ids

    What UGL().tokenizer.encode(ugl, bos=True, eos=True) returns. Each concept is one id, modifiers are separate ids, and literals are spelled out.

    Reading UGL

    • n# noun, v# verb, a# adjective/adverb, nv# verb used as a noun. Letters after the number are modifiers (v444p = ate).
    • \v# closes the scope opened by verb #. #9 is a number, 'John' a name, n?word a word not in the dictionary.
    • Order is subject, verb, adverbs, object. Modifiers follow their head. Hover or tap a token for details.
